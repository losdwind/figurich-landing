OpenClaw / NanoClaw

这是数字狂徒里最重的一层。通过 home camera、home speaker、skills 与 plugins，它会在你的真实环境里参与提醒、观察、记录和反馈，把“我知道该做什么”推到“我的环境会帮我做到”。

This is the heaviest layer in the system. With home camera, home speaker, skills, and plugins, it brings reminders, observation, tracking, and feedback into your real environment.

适合谁 Best for

你不想只靠意志力，而是想让空间、设备和 AI 一起参与健康节奏维护。

You want your space, devices, and AI to help maintain rhythm instead of relying on willpower alone.

它做什么 What it does

一整套协作式健康系统，将环境、设备与 AI 整合为统一方案。

A coherent wellbeing system that integrates environment, devices, and AI into a unified solution.

分层架构，每一层各司其职。 Layered architecture — each layer has a dedicated role.

  • home camera：在明确边界内辅助观察习惯发生与否。 home camera: helps observe whether a routine happened within clear boundaries.
  • home speaker：在合适时点做自然提醒、口头确认和节奏推动。 home speaker: handles natural reminders, spoken check-ins, and momentum cues.
  • skills 与 plugins：把 habit tracking、日志记录、健康提示和工作流串起来。 skills and plugins: connect habit tracking, logging, health prompts, and workflow orchestration.

让反馈在生活里出现，而不是只在复盘时才出现。 Move feedback into life instead of waiting for reflection time.

  • 早晨：根据你的设定做启动提醒和状态确认。 Morning: startup reminders and simple status confirmation.
  • 白天：在你容易掉线的时段做交互式提醒与习惯追踪。 Daytime: interactive reminders and habit tracking during drift-prone hours.
  • 晚上：用更节制的方式给你健康 insights 和复盘建议。 Evening: restrained health insights and reflection prompts.

前提是它必须可控、可解释，而且真的服务于健康目标。 It only works if it stays controllable, explainable, and clearly in service of health goals.

  • 只在约定场景里启用交互与观察，不做无边界的持续监控。 Interaction and observation happen only in agreed contexts, not as open-ended surveillance.
  • 先定义你想追踪的习惯，再决定哪些设备参与。 Define the habits first, then decide which devices should participate.
  • 所有 insight 都应该能解释清楚“为什么这样判断”。 Every insight should be explainable in terms of why the system reached that conclusion.

先聊清楚你的空间、习惯目标和设备边界，再决定具体怎么配。 Start by clarifying your space, target habits, and device boundaries before deciding the exact setup.
